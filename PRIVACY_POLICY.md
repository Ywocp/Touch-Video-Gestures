# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy applies to **Touch Video Gestures: Touch Gestures for Video**（触控视频手势）.

1. Touch Video Gestures: Touch Gestures for Video does not collect any personal information.

2. Touch Video Gestures: Touch Gestures for Video does not connect to the internet. It makes no network requests of any kind — no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no update checks, no advertising.

3. All gesture recognition runs locally in your browser. Touch coordinates and video state (playback time, volume, playback rate, fullscreen status) are read in memory and discarded immediately. Nothing is recorded, stored, or transmitted.

4. The only data the extension saves is the settings you configure yourself (gesture toggles, sensitivity, thresholds, toast size and position). These are stored by your browser via `chrome.storage.sync`, or in `localStorage` if the former is unavailable. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.

5. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, for advertising or any other purpose.

6. The extension's code is fully contained in the published package. It does not load or execute remote code, and does not use `eval()` or `new Function()`.

7. The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 中文版

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 6 日

1. Touch Video Gestures：触控视频手势不收集任何个人信息。

2. 本扩展不联网，不发起任何形式的网络请求——没有统计分析、没有数据上报、没有崩溃收集、没有版本检查、没有广告。

3. 全部手势识别均在浏览器本地完成。触摸坐标与视频状态（播放时间、音量、播放速率、全屏状态）仅在内存中读取并即时丢弃，不作记录、不作存储、不作传输。

4. 本扩展唯一保存的数据，是你自己设置的偏好参数（各手势开关、灵敏度、阈值、提示框字号与位置）。这些数据由浏览器通过 `chrome.storage.sync` 保存，该接口不可用时存入 `localStorage`。卸载扩展即被删除。

5. 不向任何第三方出售、共享或转让任何数据，不用于广告，也不用于任何其他目的。

6. 本扩展的全部代码均包含在已发布的安装包内，不加载、不执行远程代码，亦不使用 `eval()` 或 `new Function()`。

7. 本扩展对自 Google API 获取的信息的使用，将遵守 [Chrome 网上应用店用户数据政策](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use)，包括其中的有限使用（Limited Use）要求。
